Your first visit

Bring the question you most want answered. Your osteopath will begin by listening before asking you to move or considering hands-on care.

Before you arrive

Request an initial consultation and wait for reception to confirm your time. Bring any relevant reports you already have and a list of questions.

What to wear

A comfortable top and trousers or shorts usually work well. If a movement or position is uncomfortable, tell your osteopath.

During the assessment

Your osteopath will explain what they would like to assess and why. You can ask questions, decline a technique or pause at any time.

Before you leave

Go over what you noticed during the assessment, discuss the options and agree on your next step. A follow-up can be requested if you and your osteopath decide it would be helpful.
