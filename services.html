Osteopathy appointments

Start with a consultation if you’re new to the practice. Choose a focused assessment for a specific movement concern, or a follow-up if you’re already working with an osteopath.

Initial osteopathy consultation

For a first appointment. Tell us when the discomfort began, what makes it easier or harder, and what you want to get back to.

Your osteopath will explain the assessment and discuss any proposed hands-on care with you before proceeding.

Movement assessment

For a particular movement that feels stiff or uncomfortable, such as reaching for a shelf or turning to look behind you.

Your osteopath may ask you to repeat a movement at your own pace and describe where it feels restricted. Together you can discuss what to do next.

Request a visit

Follow-up osteopathy

For returning patients. Review what has changed since your last appointment and discuss the next step with your osteopath.

You can revisit a movement you tried last time and discuss whether the plan still fits your daily activities.
