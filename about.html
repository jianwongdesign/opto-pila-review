About OsteoMotion

OsteoMotion is an osteopathy and rehabilitation practice. We start with something you want to do more comfortably, whether that’s putting on a jacket or reaching the top shelf. That everyday detail gives us a place to begin your assessment.

Inside a consultation

Imagine reaching up to a kitchen shelf has become uncomfortable. Explore how we might work through that with you.

Tell us what you notice

Is it lifting your arm, holding it up or lowering the mug that bothers you? We ask about that moment and which other activities feel different. Your own words help us decide what to look at together.

Look at the movement together

We might ask you to show us the reach at a comfortable pace. Before any hands-on assessment, your osteopath explains what they’d like to check and asks your permission. You can pause or ask for another approach.

Agree on a next step

We explain what we noticed and discuss the options with you. If you come back, that same reach gives us something familiar to compare. You can tell us what feels easier and what still gets in the way.

Osteopathy and rehabilitation at our practice

During the consultation

Your osteopath looks at how you move and explains what they find. If they suggest hands-on care, you can ask what it involves and decide whether to go ahead.

Between visits

A rehabilitation goal can be as ordinary as getting dressed more comfortably. Tell us what you want to get back to and what fits into your day, so you can agree on a realistic next step.

Coming in for the first time?

Bring a question or a movement you’d like us to look at. A first consultation gives us time to talk it through.
